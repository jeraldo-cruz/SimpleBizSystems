Field Dispatch Board

A lightweight operational dispatch console built for Hill Country plumbers, HVAC technicians, electricians, and septic contractors. Monitor live truck locations, sequential arrival ETAs, and print shift manifests with zero per-user subscription fees.

How to Operate the Field Dispatch Board

Step 1
Log Service Calls

Enter customer details, select the service city, and assign directly to a truck or stage in the unassigned queue.

Step 2
Color Code Trucks

Click the color circle beside any driver to choose a custom vehicle color. Map pins and routes update instantly.

Step 3
Expand Routes & ETAs

Click any technician card to expand their active job, upcoming queued call, and auto-calculated sequential arrival time.

Step 4
Export Shift Manifest

Click "Export Shift PDF" at the end of the day to generate a branded log sheet with mileage and technician signoffs.
