Why build on Google tools?

If your business has a free Gmail account or a basic Google Workspace email, you already own one of the most powerful database engines in the world. Google Sheets, Google Forms, and Google Drive are fast, reliable, and completely private to your account.

Instead of buying bloated software suites, we build lightweight custom add-ons, phone-friendly screens, and automated spreadsheets directly into your Google account. You get exactly what your business needs to quote jobs, dispatch workers, and log leads—without paying monthly software bills.
