About Simple Biz Systems

Helping independent trade contractors and local shops thrive with basic software systems, lean workflows, and free or low-cost website help.

Jeraldo Cruz

Founder • Operations & Systems Specialist
Connect on LinkedIn

I started Simple Biz Systems with a clear purpose: to make sure local businesses thrive as Kingsland and the Highland Lakes continue to grow.

With a background spanning network operations, technical support engineering, and operations management, I have spent years diagnosing why technology often gets in the way of real work instead of speeding it up. Big software corporations build bloated apps that charge local trade contractors $50 to $150 every month per user for features they never touch in the field.

We do things differently. We build basic software systems tailored to the exact daily rhythm of independent plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, and service contractors. Our tools connect directly to the platforms you already own, like your private Google Workspace, with zero monthly per-seat licensing fees.

Beyond custom business tools, I maintain The Kingsland Wire as a free civic utility and provide free or low-cost website and technical support for independent shops and contractors across Kingsland, Marble Falls, Llano, and Burnet.
