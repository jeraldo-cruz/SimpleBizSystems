About Simple Biz Systems

Building reliable, subscription-free operating systems for trade contractors and small shops across the Texas Hill Country.

The Problem With Modern Business Software

Too many contractors, technicians, and local owners are trapped paying $150 to $400 every single month for bloated SaaS platforms. You pay for dozens of features you never touch, deal with complex interfaces your crew hates using, and the moment you want to cancel, they hold your customer records and business history hostage.

Simple Biz Systems was founded on a simple principle: If your shop already has a Google account, you already own all the backend technology you will ever need. You don't need another recurring bill—you just need practical, custom-built tools that do the job cleanly.

Behind the Systems

I'm Jeraldo Cruz, and I live right here in Kingsland, Texas. My background is in enterprise operations and systems infrastructure. Over the years, I've managed dispatch workflows, backend user access, and technical operations for fast-moving companies where speed, uptime, and clarity meant everything.

I created Simple Biz Systems to bring that same high-level operational efficiency directly to the trades and independent shops around Lake LBJ, Marble Falls, and Llano—without the Silicon Valley price tags or complex setups.
