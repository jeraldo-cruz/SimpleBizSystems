Frequently Asked Questions on Job Estimates

How do you calculate a contractor job estimate?

To calculate an accurate job estimate, determine your Direct Material Costs and add your Estimated Labor (total job hours multiplied by your hourly billing rate). Combine these to establish your Direct Job Cost. Finally, divide by your gross margin factor (e.g., divide by 0.70 for a 30% gross profit margin) to ensure overhead expenses and net earnings are covered before applying local sales tax.

How much markup should a contractor charge on materials?

Contractors typically charge between 15% and 35% markup on wholesale materials to cover transport, waste, procurement time, and material handling liability. Be sure not to confuse markup with margin: a 30% markup on a $1,000 material purchase yields $300 profit, which equals only a 23% gross margin on the final invoice.
