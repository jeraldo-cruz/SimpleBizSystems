Frequently Asked Questions on Utility Onboarding & Document Verification

What documents are required for utility onboarding in the Highland Lakes?

For electric onboarding (CTEC) or water onboarding (KWSC/KMUD), providers require a government-issued photo ID, proof of property ownership (recorded warranty deed) or signed lease agreement, a certified 911 physical county address verification, and applicable meter deposit or transfer fees. PO Boxes are strictly rejected for physical service establishment.

Why do utility companies reject PO Box addresses during onboarding?

Electric cooperatives and municipal water districts require a physical 911 county road address to locate utility easements, physical transformer drops, and meter loops. A PO Box is only acceptable as a secondary billing address after the physical service connection is established.

Is my personal identification data stored when using this checklist?
