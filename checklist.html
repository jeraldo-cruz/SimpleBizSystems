Critical Document Readiness Checklist

Why this tool exists: Applications for Texas food benefits, utility relief, and emergency county care frequently stall for weeks due to a single missing paper or an unverified physical address. This tool lets you verify your documentation before submitting your packet, avoiding delays and repeat trips. Everything is processed privately inside your device browser with zero cloud storage.
