Section 3 County Public Safety & Fire Restrictions

Section 4 Community Directory & Public Services

Local Directory by Category

Free Public Access

Kingsland Lions Memorial Park & Boat Ramp

Free public boat ramp, fishing pier, shaded pavilions, and community docks located on Lake LBJ (Eula Rough Rd):

  • Ramp Access: Paved dual-lane ramp open 24/7.
  • Parking: Truck and trailer parking available on site.
  • Pavilion Rentals: Coordinated through Kingsland MUD.
Directions (Google Maps) → Free Public Facility
Swimming & River Bed

The Slab (Llano River Crossing)

Famous granite riverbed crossing on FM 3404 where the Llano River flows over natural rock formations:

  • Activities: Wading, shallow swimming, and sunbathing.
  • Access: Free roadside public access on FM 3404.
  • Safety Note: Check river flows after heavy rains.
Directions (Google Maps) → Public Crossing
