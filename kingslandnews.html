Community Directory & County Resources

Public Access

Free Parks, Boat Ramps & The Slab

Public lake access points and parks in and around Kingsland with zero admission fees:

  • Kingsland Lions Park: Public boat ramp and dock on Lake LBJ (Eula Rough Rd).
  • The Slab (Llano River): Historic granite riverbed crossing off FM 3404.
  • Kingsland Community Park: Playgrounds and shaded grounds on Nob Hill.
  • Kingsland Branch Library: 125 W Polk St (Free Wi-Fi and printing).
  • Inks Dam National Fish Hatchery: Walking trails and shoreline access.

Safety & Regulations

County Government & Burn Bans

Kingsland spans both Llano and Burnet Counties. Outdoor burning restrictions must be checked with your specific county dispatch:

  • Llano County: Oversees western Kingsland and Pack Saddle Mountain.
  • Burnet County: Oversees eastern Kingsland and Lake Victor corridor.
  • Emergency Dispatch: Check burning status before lighting any brush piles.
Llano County Portal → Dispatch: 325-247-5767
Burnet County Portal → Dispatch: 512-756-8080
Power & Outages

Central Texas Electric Co-op (CTEC)

Member-owned electric co-op serving rural Kingsland and the Highland Lakes basin. Online billing and emergency outage reporting:

  • SmartHub Portal: View hourly energy use and pay power bills.
  • Outage Map: Real-time substation and feeder outage monitoring.
CTEC SmartHub Portal → Outages: 800-900-2832
Water & Wastewater

Kingsland MUD

Municipal utility district providing retail water, sanitary sewer, and community brush drop-off for Kingsland:

  • District Office: 100 Ingram St, Kingsland, TX 78639.
  • Brush Site: Monthly resident brush and wood recycling disposal.
