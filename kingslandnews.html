Free Public Spots Details ▾

Free Parks, Boat Ramps & The Slab

Public lake access, community parks, library, and the Llano River Slab.

Free public spots in and around Kingsland with zero admission fees:
  • Kingsland Lions Park: Public boat ramp & dock on Lake LBJ (Eula Rough Rd).
  • The Slab (Llano River): Historic river crossing & shallow swimming off FM 3404.
  • Kingsland Community Park: Playgrounds & park grounds on Nob Hill.
  • Kingsland Branch Library: 125 W Polk St (Free Wi-Fi, computers, and printing).
  • Inks Dam National Fish Hatchery: Public walking trails & shoreline access.
