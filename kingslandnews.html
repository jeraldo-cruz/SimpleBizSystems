About The Kingsland Wire

The Kingsland Wire is an independent community resource dedicated to free and low-cost services, public amenities, and essential utilities across Kingsland, Llano County, and Burnet County.

Most commercial news websites are crowded with subscription pop-ups and tracking cookies. We built this board to be completely ad-free, subscription-free, and privacy-respecting. It pulls directly from open government records—including the National Weather Service, Texas Water Development Board, and county emergency management—refreshing automatically so local residents always have straightforward, honest information at their fingertips.
