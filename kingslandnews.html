Burn Ban Orders (Llano & Burnet)

Active outdoor burning restrictions for residential brush and yard waste.

Rule: Outdoor burning of brush, trash, or debris is prohibited during active county declarations.
Enforcement: Class C misdemeanor citations ($500 max fine).
  • Llano County Dispatch: 325-247-5767

  • BBQ grills and enclosed fire pits for cooking are generally permitted with spark arrestors.

Local Community Questions & Resources

Answers for Kingsland & Highland Lakes
What days is Share the Harvest Food Pantry open in Kingsland?

Share the Harvest Food Pantry (located at 3435 RM 1431) is open on Thursday mornings from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM for food distributions. Local families in need can register on-site to receive groceries, produce, and household necessities.

Where does the ARC or Central Texas mobile food truck stop in Llano County?

Mobile pantry distributions rotate across community centers and church grounds in Kingsland, Llano, and Marble Falls. These events operate on a drive-through basis where pre-sorted fresh produce and staple food boxes are loaded directly into cars.

How do I know if I am in Llano County or Burnet County in Kingsland?

Kingsland is divided by the Colorado River arm of Lake LBJ. Generally, areas west of the river and south toward Sunrise Beach sit within Llano County, while areas northeast across the bridge and toward Hoover Valley sit within Burnet County. Burn ban rules follow each respective county commissioner's court.
