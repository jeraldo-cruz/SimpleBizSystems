About The Kingsland Wire

The Kingsland Wire is a free community utility for Kingsland, Texas, and surrounding Highland Lakes communities in Llano and Burnet Counties.

Unlike commercial news sites, this bulletin is 100% ad-free, subscription-free, and has zero tracking cookies. It aggregates open government data, county emergency notices, free public boat ramps, and essential utility contacts into one fast-loading page for local residents and visitors.
